Consultant ecologist: mid-grade pay and licences

Consultant Ecologist (mid-grade) sits between Assistant and Senior. The role is independent on PEAs and most protected-species survey work but typically not yet leading EIA chapters. Protected-species licences are the single biggest pay variable at this grade.

◇ Oliver Wakefield-Smith · Refreshed June 2026

Briefing note · Direct answer

Consultant Ecologist UK median in 2024 was £32,400 (CIEEM Survey, GradCIEEM-ACIEEM cut). The band sits £29,000-£38,000 across consultancy tiers. Bat-licence holders (Class 1+) and GCN survey leaders typically earn £2,000-£4,000 above the base band; dormouse and badger licences add modestly.
◇ Source: CIEEM Salary & Conditions Survey 2024 + Natural England licensing register + CJS Pay & Conditions 2024

Day-to-day duties

  • PEA (Preliminary Ecological Appraisal) authorship and project leadership.
  • Protected-species surveys (bat emergence/re-entry, GCN HSI and eDNA, dormouse tube checks, badger sett surveys, reptile mat surveys).
  • BNG metric calculation and reporting under Defra statutory metric.
  • Junior staff mentoring and report QA support for the Senior tier.

Licence value

Natural England Class licences for bat (1, 2, 3, 4), GCN (Class 1) and dormouse (Class 1 / individual) extend the work a Consultant can lead independently and materially affect billable rate. Most consultancies pay a licence-bonus or set a higher band cell for chartered licence-holders.

BNG impact at this grade

BNG metric calculation has shifted from specialist to standard for Consultant grade since 2024. Familiarity with the Defra statutory metric, UKHab and BNG record-keeping is effectively expected on all advertised Consultant posts in 2026.
