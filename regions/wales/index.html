Conservation officer salary in Wales

Wales is dominated by Natural Resources Wales (NRW) on the public side, Wildlife Trusts Wales, RSPB Cymru and a smaller heritage cohort. Welsh local authority CO pay is on the NJC 2025/26 spine (same as England); Welsh income tax remains UK-aligned for 2026/27.

◇ Oliver Wakefield-Smith · Refreshed June 2026

Briefing note · Direct answer

NRW pays an Adviser (B-band) about £28,500-£32,500 in 2026; a Specialist Adviser (C-band) £33,500-£40,000. Welsh local authority conservation officers sit on NJC SCP 23-33, identical to English counterparts. Welsh-rate income tax is set to the UK rates plus zero in 2026/27.
◇ Source: Natural Resources Wales careers + NJC pay spine 2025/26 (LGE) + Welsh Government workforce strategy

Natural Resources Wales (NRW)

NRW is the principal public-sector employer for nature-conservation specialists in Wales, with about 2,000 staff (NRW Annual Report 2023/24). It runs its own banded pay framework (A through F) parallel to the UK Civil Service grades, with B-band approximately equivalent to EO and D-band to SEO.

Welsh local authority CO pay

The 22 Welsh local authorities use the NJC pay spine identically to England and apply the same London-weighting-free regional supplements (NJC schedule). Wildlife Trusts Wales and RSPB Cymru follow their parent body's federation pay structure.

Take-home in Wales

For 2026/27, the Welsh Government has set the Welsh rate of income tax to align with UK rates (10 percent reserved + 10 percent Welsh + 5 percent higher set to make basic 20 / higher 40 / additional 45). Take-home is therefore identical to England at the same gross.
