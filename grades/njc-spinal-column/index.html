NJC pay spine for conservation officers

The NJC (National Joint Council) spinal column runs from SCP 3 to SCP 43 (SCP 1 was deleted in April 2023 and SCP 2 from 1 April 2026); conservation officers in local authorities typically sit SCP 18 to SCP 38. The point values below are the 2025/26 spine; the 2026/27 award of 3.30 percent was agreed in August 2026 (after UNISON's strike ballot closed on 6 August 2026 below the turnout threshold for industrial action) and applies from 1 April 2026, backdated, so current pay is the 2025/26 point plus 3.30 percent.

◇ Oliver Wakefield-Smith · Refreshed June 2026

Briefing note · Direct answer

NJC 2025/26 points relevant to conservation officers: SCP 18 £31,537 (Assistant CO entry), SCP 23 £34,434 (CO mid-band), SCP 28 £39,152 (Senior CO), SCP 33 £44,075 (Principal CO / Team Leader), SCP 38 £49,282 (Team Manager). The 2026/27 award of 3.30 percent was agreed in August 2026 and applies from 1 April 2026 (backdated), so add 3.30 percent for current pay: for example SCP 23 rises from £34,434 to about £35,570 and SCP 28 from £39,152 to about £40,444. SCP 2 was deleted from the spine on 1 April 2026. London boroughs pay on separate Inner/Outer London pay spines; the effective uplift over the national point at these grades runs roughly £4,000 to £6,000 (Inner) and £2,900 to £4,000 (Outer). Where a borough instead applies the GLPC separate London allowance it is £4,319 (Inner) or £2,301 (Outer) from April 2025.
◇ Source: NJC pay spine 2025/26 (LGE published schedule) + 2026/27 NJC pay agreement (3.30%, agreed August 2026)

Typical SCP ranges for the role

  • Assistant Conservation Officer: SCP 18-22
  • Conservation Officer (planning or nature): SCP 23-28
  • Senior Conservation Officer / Senior Planning CO: SCP 28-33
  • Principal / Team Leader: SCP 33-38
  • Conservation Team Manager: SCP 38-43

How SCP is applied

Most authorities use a spot-point or narrow-banded application of NJC (e.g. "SCP 23-26 on appointment, progression to SCP 28 over 3-5 years subject to performance"). A few use broad-banded grades that span 5-8 SCPs without automatic increment progression.

Market supplements

Where recruitment is difficult (often planning conservation officer roles in inner-London boroughs with backlog), authorities apply a market supplement on top of SCP, typically £2,000-£5,000. Market supplements are time-limited and reviewable; they are not a permanent uplift on the spine.
