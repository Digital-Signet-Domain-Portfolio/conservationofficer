§ REGIONS & GRADES

Civil Service SEO pay for senior conservation roles

SEO (Senior Executive Officer) is the typical grade for principal specialists and small team leads in Defra ALBs. SEO conservation roles often align with CIEEM Full Member (MCIEEM) or Chartered Ecologist (CEcol).

◇ Oliver Wakefield-Smith · Refreshed June 2026

Briefing note · Direct answer

SEO conservation roles paid £40,000-£47,000 outside London in 2026/27 across Defra ALBs. Defra core SEO sat at £44,000-£52,000. Inner London adds c.£4,000. Most post-holders are MCIEEM with 8+ years' relevant experience; CEcol is increasingly expected for principal-technical posts.
◇ Source: Defra grade pay & Civil Service main pay 2026/27 + CIEEM Salary & Conditions Survey 2024 (principal-grade cut)

SEO conservation role mix

Typical SEO conservation titles: Principal Adviser, Principal Specialist, Lead Ecologist, Senior Casework Lead. SEO often holds team-lead responsibility for 3-7 EO/HEO direct reports, or principal-technical authority for a specialism (e.g. EPS licensing).

CIEEM Full Member / Chartered alignment

The 2024 CIEEM Salary Survey shows MCIEEM (Full Members) cluster between £36,000 and £52,000 across sectors; chartered (CEcol) cluster between £44,000 and £62,000. The SEO band aligns well with the MCIEEM and CEcol mid-points, particularly in public-sector employers.

HEO to SEO timeline

Typical HEO to SEO timescale in a Defra ALB is 3-5 years for someone with a strong technical track record; longer in policy-only careers because SEO policy roles compete with cross-government strands. SEO recruitment is competitive but the Defra ALB cohort regularly externally advertises.
