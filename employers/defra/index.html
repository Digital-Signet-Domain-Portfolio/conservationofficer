Briefing note · Direct answer

Defra’s national minimum salaries are EO £29,715, HEO £34,765, SEO £42,665, Grade 7 £56,375 and Grade 6 £67,730 (Defra group grades page, read 16 November 2026), including the August 2025 pay award. Each grade runs up to a maximum, and London pays more: on Defra’s 2024/25 pay table the HEO range was £33,590 to £36,000 nationally and £37,480 to £40,170 in London. The 2026/27 pay round is still being negotiated.

The 'from' figures above are the national minimum salaries on the Defra group grades page (Civil Service Careers, read 16 November 2026); they include the August 2025 pay award. Each grade runs from a minimum to a maximum, and Defra publishes separate National and London pay ranges: on Defra's 2024/25 pay table (Defra Bargaining Unit formal pay offer, Table 1) the HEO range was £33,590 to £36,000 nationally and £37,480 to £40,170 in London. Arms-length bodies such as Natural England and Forestry England set their own pay ranges, so check each advert. The 2026/27 Civil Service pay remit guidance (Cabinet Office, published 21 May 2026) sets a maximum 3.5 percent increase in remuneration cost for grades below the Senior Civil Service, within which each department targets its own award; National Living Wage upratings for the lowest paid fall outside that cap.

The Defra Graduate Scheme is recruited via the Civil Service Fast Stream (Defra and ALB strand), with a starting salary of about £30,000 (London) for entry from 2026, rising on completion. Direct entry at HEO for a conservation MSc is competitive but realistic; SEO and above usually require named experience.

Conservation specialist allowances (under the Civil Service DDaT and STEM frameworks) can add a pay supplement where Defra accepts a market-supplement case for ecology, hydrology or GIS skills; the amount is set per case and stated on the advert. The structural feature to plan for is the alpha pension: 28.97 percent employer contribution makes a Defra HEO meaningfully ahead of a consultancy Senior on a like-for-like cash-equivalent.
