The Environment Agency runs its own grade structure of Staff Grades 1 to 7 alongside separate Environment Officer grades (Environment Officer A and B) for operational and field conservation work, plus Field Operations, Graduate and Nuclear grades (EA Pay Offer 2025). It is not a Civil Service band structure, and the EA does not publish its grade salary tables publicly, so the ranges above are indicative and benchmarked to ONS ASHE 2025 and the Icon and CJS sector surveys rather than lifted from an EA pay scale. The EA employs about 11,000 staff (EA Annual Report 2023/24) with conservation responsibilities concentrated in Environment Officer and biodiversity-specialist roles.

The most recent verified award is the 2025 pay offer: a consolidated increase of 4 percent of grade rate for Staff Grades 3 and 4 and Environment Officer B, 5 percent for Staff Grades 1 and 2, and a total 3.9 percent for Staff Grades 5 to 7, all effective from 1 July 2025 and running to 30 June 2026 (EA Pay Offer 2025, government pay remit approved 16 July 2025). Awards are backdated to 1 July or appointment date and are pensionable. That award period has now ended; the 2026/27 EA pay round was still in negotiation as of September 2026, with the joint trade unions and the Agency in talks and no new offer yet announced, so EA rates remain at the 2025-awarded levels pending settlement.

EA conservation posts are more often permanent than the wider sector: the EA vacancy sample we analysed (Jan-Apr 2026, gov.uk listings) ran a fixed-term ratio of about 18 percent, well below RSPB or Wildlife Trust equivalents. That stability translates into materially higher cash-equivalent total reward over a five-year horizon. Environment Agency staff are not civil servants and are not in the Civil Service alpha pension: they belong to the Environment Agency Pension Fund, a defined-benefit Local Government Pension Scheme fund (EAPF, established under SI 1996/711).

Questions this page answers

What are the Environment Agency Staff Grades and Environment Officer grades, and what do they pay?

The Environment Agency uses Staff Grades 1 to 7 plus separate Environment Officer grades (A and B) for operational and field conservation work, not Civil Service bands. The EA does not publish its grade salary tables publicly; indicative ranges for conservation roles run about £27,000 to £70,000, benchmarked to ONS ASHE 2025 and sector surveys.

What does an EA Environment Officer (conservation) typically earn?

An Environment Officer, the common conservation entry grade, sits at an indicative reported range of about £30,500 to £35,500 in 2026. The EA does not publish an official rate for the grade, so this is a benchmark from ONS ASHE and sector salary data, not an EA pay scale figure.

What was the 2025 Environment Agency pay award for Staff Grades 3 and 4?

Under the 2025 pay offer, Staff Grades 3 and 4 and Environment Officer B received a consolidated increase of 4 percent of grade rate, effective 1 July 2025 and running to 30 June 2026. Staff Grades 1 and 2 received 5 percent, and Staff Grades 5 to 7 a total of 3.9 percent (EA Pay Offer 2025).

Which pension scheme do Environment Agency staff belong to?

Environment Agency staff are not civil servants and are not in the Civil Service alpha pension. They belong to the Environment Agency Pension Fund, a defined-benefit Local Government Pension Scheme fund established under SI 1996/711.

Are EA conservation roles more often permanent than the wider sector?

Yes. The EA vacancy sample analysed for this fieldbook (Jan-Apr 2026, gov.uk listings) ran a fixed-term ratio of about 18 percent, well below RSPB or Wildlife Trust equivalents, which raises cash-equivalent total reward over a five-year horizon.
