Briefing note · Direct answer · the 40-60 word lede

A UK conservation officer salary in 2026 ranges roughly £22,000 to £42,000. NGO entry posts at the RSPB and Wildlife Trusts sit at the lower end (£22k-£26k), Defra arms-length bodies and senior local authority posts at the upper end. About half of advertised posts are fixed-term: the sector's headline number is held down by its FTC dependence, not by the median rate of pay.
◇ Source: ONS ASHE 2025 Table 14.6a (SOC 1158) + Icon Salary Survey 2024 + CJS Pay & Conditions 2024
