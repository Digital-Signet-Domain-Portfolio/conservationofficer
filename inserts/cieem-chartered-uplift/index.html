CIEEM chartered uplift: what each grade is worth

CIEEM grades carry measurable salary uplift. ACIEEM to MCIEEM is the larger step; MCIEEM to CEcol the more selective. CEnv adds breadth rather than depth.

◇ Oliver Wakefield-Smith · Refreshed June 2026

Briefing note · Direct answer

CIEEM 2024 survey medians by grade: ACIEEM £32,000, MCIEEM £45,000, MCIEEM with CEcol £53,000. The ACIEEM to MCIEEM step is about £8,000-£13,000; MCIEEM to CEcol about £5,000-£10,000. 2026 annual fees: Associate £276, Full £312. CEcol application fee separate.
◇ Source: CIEEM Salary & Conditions Survey 2024 + CIEEM membership grades and fees 2026 + Society for the Environment CEnv register

Evidence required at each grade

  • ACIEEM: assessed competence in four core areas via portfolio and peer-review interview. Typically 3-5 years' relevant experience post-BSc.
  • MCIEEM: assessed competence to lead work and supervise others. Portfolio across full project lifecycles, evidence of independent technical judgement. Typically 6-10 years' relevant experience.
  • CEcol: chartered panel assessment focused on ecological practice. Demonstration of principal-technical competence and contribution to the profession. Typically MCIEEM + 2-3 years.
  • CEnv: chartered panel assessment of broader environmental management competence via CIEEM or another Soc Env licensed body.

Typical timelines

BSc to ACIEEM: 3-5 years. ACIEEM to MCIEEM: 4-7 years. MCIEEM to CEcol: 2-4 years. The full BSc-to-CEcol path typically takes 10-14 years of consistent professional development.

Does CEnv add value beyond CEcol?

CEnv adds breadth recognition (environmental management) and is useful for consultants moving into multi-disciplinary roles or for those pitching to CEnv-recognising clients. It rarely adds salary on top of CEcol but can support a Principal-grade career-move into broader environmental practice.
